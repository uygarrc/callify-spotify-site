Introducing Callify

and synced in minutes.

Every call ends with the same admin: notes, a deal score, a HubSpot update and a Slack recap. Callify does it in about four minutes, so each rep gets roughly 5 hours a week back.

How it works

Callify sits between the tools you already use.

Gong, HubSpot and Slack stay exactly as they are. Callify reads the call, scores it against your playbook and writes the results back.

A layer on your current stack. No rip-and-replace.

After every call · step by step

  1. 4:47

    Gong records the call

    The call ends. Gong already has the recording and transcript.

  2. 4:48

    Callify pulls the transcript

    Read-only, straight from Gong. Nothing to upload.

  3. 4:49

    Scores it against your playbook

    MEDDIC, SPICED, BANT or your own, with gaps flagged.

  4. 4:50

    Drafts HubSpot + Slack

    Deal notes, stage and next step, plus a short recap.

  5. 4:51

    You review and approve

    Nothing is sent until the rep says so.

  6. 4:51

    HubSpot and Slack update

    Deal updated, recap posted. Laptop closed.
